What to Track in a Lead Reactivation Campaign

Expenses & Spend Control

Reactivation is about useful conversations. Track enough of the journey to understand where people engage, what they need, and whether the next step is being handled.

Start with a clear campaign goal

Decide what progress looks like for this group of leads. It may be learning whether the need still exists, identifying a suitable opportunity, or arranging a call with your team.

Look beyond message counts

Outreach volume does not explain conversation quality. Review replies, qualification context, handoffs, tasks, and bookings alongside the messages sent.

Use the numbers to ask better questions

If replies stop at a particular question, read the exchanges. If interested leads are waiting on your team, improve the handoff. Let the dashboard point you back to the work that matters.

Bring it back to your business

SmartAi Sales starts with your existing leads, your approved knowledge, and your team’s way of working. A walkthrough is a chance to explore how this approach could fit your sales process.
